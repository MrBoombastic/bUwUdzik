Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the clOwOck mobile application designed for the Qingping CGD1 alarm clock.

2. Data Collection and Usage

clOwOck is designed to operate locally on your device. We prioritize your privacy by strictly limiting data collection:

  • Bluetooth & Location Data: The app requires Bluetooth and Location permissions to scan for and connect to your Qingping alarm clock. This data is processed locally and is never transmitted to any external servers.
  • Sensor Data: Temperature, humidity, and battery readings fetched from your device are displayed locally within the app and on your home screen widgets. This data is not stored permanently or shared with third parties.
  • Alarm and Settings: Your alarm schedules, custom ringtones, and device preferences are stored locally on your device and synced directly with your Qingping CGD1 via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any built-in third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks. If you choose to use the Custom Ringtone JSON Manifest feature, the app will make standard HTTP(S) requests to the URL you provide to fetch ringtone data. The privacy practices of those external URLs are beyond our control.

4. Permissions Required

  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: Required to find, connect to, and communicate with the Qingping alarm clock.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display foreground service notifications when maintaining background connections or data updates.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by creating an issue on the GitHub repository or by sending an e-mail.
